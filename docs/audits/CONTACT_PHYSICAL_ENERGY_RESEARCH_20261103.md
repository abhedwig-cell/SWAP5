# Contact and physical heat-transport research contract

Status: **Proposed research implementation, not production admission.**

Base is canonical2924fefac. Two bounded executable advances are selected before
changing an original physical law. Current source and historical reference
behaviour remain available unchanged. The full scientific denominator stays0/3.

Andelst: replay the archived actual112-node failed Newton system, with its
measured off/on absorption jump and node26/27 contact. Use the real production
tridiagonal solver and an additional contact unknown. Check whether both
ordinary branch corrections contradict their own topology, and whether a
bounded contact fraction can satisfy the linearized residual and contact
constraint simultaneously. This is a proposed set-valued contact completion,
not proof of a nonlinear accepted step, macro storage update, restart or481-day
solution. A successful witness must next be tested in the actual coupled
nonlinear owner before promotion. Never widen a mass tolerance or silently
smooth the original entry law.

Energy: an isolated finite-volume warm-soil research solver will combine the
actual production linear-mixture stock law with explicit liquid and counterflow
gas enthalpy, root extraction/replacement gas and prescribed conductive/net
thermal energy. Internal face transfers must cancel exactly in the column
ledger. Water amounts are inputs with a separately verified nodal balance;
they are never inferred from a heat residual. Unknown external donor
temperatures and unknown boundary heat are refusals, including reverse flow.
Only exact zero transport can dispense with its donor. Gauge changes must not
change the solved physical temperature. Inputs and committed state are read-only;
rejected trials publish no temperature candidate.

This solver assumes fixed porosity and constant phase heat capacities. Water
and gas face/source volumes are supplied independently and must each close
their own nodal volume balance. The solver does not invent counterflow gas;
the manufactured tests may explicitly prescribe it as a connected-gas example.
Actual gas flow and donor receipts need a separately justified provider. Ice, vapor,
snow and mechanical/chemical energy are excluded. Radiation and latent energy
can enter only as explicitly supplied boundary receipts; their atmospheric
owners are not implemented by this experiment. The full SWAP physical-energy
flag therefore remains false even if this bounded sensible ledger closes.
This is a new model experiment under ADR-0005, not a B1 bug correction.

Selection: small direct compilation closures at checked O0/O2; actual Andelst
matrix versus an independent high-precision solve; manufactured heat/transport
solutions, independent energy and water reconstruction, gauge invariance,
reverse-flow donor attacks and transactional refusal. Expected runtime under
two minutes. Persist implementation and status before qualification. Full
original project reruns are not selected until a coupled production route
exists; they cannot be replaced by these component tests.
