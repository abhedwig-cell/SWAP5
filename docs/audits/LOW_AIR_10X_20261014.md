## Cause verified against production

The unchanged original first highest B15 low-air case over 1e-4 day reproduces
**37791 accepted substeps /523944 temporal rejections**. A canonical substep is
one internally accepted transaction, not one Richards solve. Temporal rejection
is incremented after the full and both half advances converge and the full/half
mass gates pass, then fail the model-owned endpoint temporal error. Solver and
mass rejections have separate counters; both are zero in the observed case.
The transaction code makes three advances per attempt. Its 1685205 advances equal
3*(37791+523944), independently corroborating profiling.

After every accepted transaction the incumbent interval controller again requests
the **entire remaining interval**. Neighboring accepted states repeatedly need
approximately 14 halvings to a few 1e-9 day. These are different states and time
inputs, so blind memoization is invalid. Classified C (inefficient proposal
strategy), alongside B (deliberately severe fixture and unchanged 3e-11cm head
budget). There is no evidence here of Newton failure or a solver regression.
Normal and low-air fixtures have different hydraulic states and difficulty.
