# Drainage

Drainage is part of the admitted Status-A scientific production baseline and preservation surface. This page gives reviewers a bounded current description without replacing the capability-specific scientific and qualification records.

## Scientific role

Drainage represents admitted removal/exchange of water through configured drainage processes and therefore participates in the column water balance. Its exact formulation, parameterisation and activation remain those of the admitted production capability; F-DOC20 does not redefine drainage physics.

The important current-documentation point is that drainage is no longer an unfinished migration target. It is an admitted capability whose scientific behaviour and accounting belong to the qualified production postimage.

## Accounting and transaction semantics

Drainage contributions must remain consistent with the water-balance and state-ownership contracts that apply to the active interval. A tentative drainage contribution produced during a candidate attempt does not independently become authoritative if the surrounding attempt is rejected.

This means drainage review has two distinct questions:

1. does the process produce the admitted scientific drainage contribution; and
2. is that contribution published/accounted only through accepted execution authority?

## Review questions

A drainage-sensitive change should be checked for:

- preservation of the admitted process formulation and sign/unit conventions;
- correct inclusion in applicable interval and aggregate water balances;
- no leakage from rejected candidate state into committed accounting;
- unchanged activation/configuration semantics unless explicitly requalified;
- preservation evidence on every changed dependency surface.
