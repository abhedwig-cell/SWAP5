## Drain-down and storage correction

Drain-down starts from uniform head -3 m, with Sy=0.2, Ss=0, no recharge, no ET and only the left DRN. It runs 120 days at 0.25-day timesteps. Total storage decreases monotonically. Cumulative drain outflow is **16.9492406847 m³**. Final heads are -4.9995989 m in the drain cell and -4.5220792 m in the right-edge cell. The system has not fully drained to equilibrium by day 120.

Maximum native storage/DRN rate residual is about **3.0e-13 m³/d**; maximum cumulative native storage-loss/DRN residual is **9.2e-12 m³**, below the unchanged 1e-6 m³ volume gate.

The preceding run 37064998077 exposed an observation error: the script assumed storage = Sy × geometric saturated depth. In MODFLOW6 6.8.0 Newton STO, `sQuadraticSaturation` uses SATOMEGA=1e-6. In the interior saturation range its law is `s=(br-eps/2)/(1-eps)`, not exactly `s=br`. The resulting geometric storage-loss residual grew to 1.6949e-5 m³ even though the native package rate balance closed. The source-bound correction uses Sy × area × thickness × this saturation function, including the same function at the initial state. The original geometric residual remains in every final result row, and the complete failed-observation result is separately persisted.
