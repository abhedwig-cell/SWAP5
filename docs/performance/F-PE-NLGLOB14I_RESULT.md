## Interpretation

The absence of release is not caused by a static saturated geometry.

Nor is it caused by a retreating saturated front that has simply not reached the bottom.

The persistent saturated-mode trajectory instead redistributes water such that the lower saturated block expands from node 16 upward to node 3 while the profile as a whole loses water through the surface.

This is not automatically a physical defect. Internal redistribution can in principle increase storage in lower compartments while upper compartments lose more water.

But it invalidates a release criterion based on retreat of the saturated block.

The next bounded question is whether the saturated-block expansion is supported by physically consistent redistribution of compartment water storage, or is generated by the persistent saturated temporal formulation in a way that lacks a corresponding redistribution signal.
