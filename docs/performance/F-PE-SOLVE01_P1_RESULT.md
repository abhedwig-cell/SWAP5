## Purpose

P1 tests whether bounded discarded-trial approximation remains transactionally and numerically robust when the next groundwater head is generated by a live MODFLOW6 prepared-solve corrector instead of a scripted same-origin sequence.

The workunit compares:

- E0 exact-every-trial;
- E4 bounded cadence;
- EH bounded head-window.

Approximate convergence cannot authorize commit. Any approximate convergence point is validated by a real SWAP trial before publication.

## Endpoint and ownership

E4 and EH converge to the same accepted endpoint as E0.

All three arms report:

- final head: approximately `-0.7149999677331764 m`;
- final physical residual: approximately `-2.32e-19 m/s`;
- identical committed ledger exchange:
  approximately `-1.098019370398106e-12 m`;
- one SWAP commit;
- one ledger commit;
- preserved MODFLOW prepared-solve lifecycle.

Thus the bounded response does not alter the accepted physical endpoint or publication semantics on this live case.

## Validation behavior

Both E4 and EH trigger:

- two exact validation attempts;
- one failed validation followed by refresh/recovery;
- successful exact final validation before commit.

This is within the preregistered P1 robustness gate, which allowed one additional external iteration and one recovery validation.

The important result is therefore not speedup but survival of the coupled iteration with exact final authority preserved.

## Interpretation

The short F-GC44 live loop is not a favorable performance regime for solve elimination.

E0 converges in only two coupled iterations.

E4/EH have too little discarded-trial work available to amortize:

- the approximate-response steering;
- one additional MODFLOW iteration;
- exact validation and one recovery refresh.

Therefore P1 does not contradict the P0B result.

P0B showed large gains when the workload contains enough repeated discarded-trial SWAP solves:

- E4: about 56.45% median speedup;
- EH: about 42.70%;
- EF: about 70.15%.

P1 shows that on a two-iteration live coupled problem, bounded approximation is robust but not profitable.

This establishes a regime distinction rather than a universal speedup claim.

## Decision

`PASS_ROBUSTNESS_ADVANCE_TO_DIFFICULT_COUPLED_SCALE`

Advance E4 as primary candidate.

Retain EH only as conservative comparator.

Do not claim a live short-loop speedup.

The next authority must contain enough genuinely difficult coupled corrector work to determine whether the P0B solve-elimination gain survives:

- live MODFLOW iteration generation;
- exact final validation;
- refresh/recovery overhead;
- many SWAP columns and/or repeated coupled intervals.

No production admission is authorized by P1.
