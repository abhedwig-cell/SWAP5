# F-PE-TEMPORAL03 P1B — expanded dynamic-history budget frontier

Date: 2026-09-26

Status: `PREREGISTERED_RESEARCH`

## Trigger

P1A showed that the stationary TEMPORAL02 budget frontier does not transfer to accepted dynamic-history origins.

At |dh| = 0.001 cm, the largest P1A budget 1e-3 completed only 3/24 dynamic signed-history points.

At |dh| = 0.01 cm, 1e-2 completed only 9/24.

## Purpose

Determine whether a bounded common completion frontier exists for small dynamic correctors when the temporal budget is expanded beyond the stationary envelope.

Use all six difficult origins, both +/-10% physical history directions and both corrector signs.

For |dh| = 0.001 cm test:

- 2e-3;
- 5e-3;
- 1e-2;
- 2e-2;
- 5e-2;
- 1e-1;
- 2e-1 cm.

For |dh| = 0.01 cm test:

- 2e-2;
- 5e-2;
- 1e-1;
- 2e-1 cm.

One fresh process per point is sufficient for frontier discovery. Any selected boundary must be replicated separately.

## Measurements

- completion status;
- q for successful trials;
- dynamic predecessor derivative magnitude;
- origin mass residual.

## Decision

If a common frontier exists, preregister P1C replication at that frontier and the immediately stricter tested budget.

If no common frontier exists through 0.2 cm, stop budget escalation and move directly to oracle/indicator-scale analysis rather than increasing the absolute tolerance further.
