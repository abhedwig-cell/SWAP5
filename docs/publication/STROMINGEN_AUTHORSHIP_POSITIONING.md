# Stromingen authorship and editorial positioning

Date: 2026-09-19

## Recommended article type

Position the contribution as a Dutch-language overview / development article for the hydrological community, not as a research paper reporting new SWAP5 scientific results.

The article should have one coherent editorial voice and should not read as a consortium progress report.

## Recommended perspective

Use a neutral first-person-free narrative in the main text wherever possible:

- prefer: `SWAP5 wordt ontwikkeld ...`
- prefer: `In dit artikel wordt ...`
- prefer: `De huidige kwalificatie omvat ...`
- avoid: `wij hebben aangetoond ...`
- avoid: `ons nieuwe model ...`

This reduces ambiguity when the final author group includes contributors from more than one organisation and prevents development claims from sounding like manuscript novelty claims.

## Authorship model

Recommended structure:

1. one clear penvoerder / first author responsible for the integrated argument and final text;
2. a small number of co-authors who made substantive intellectual or technical contributions to the development described in the article;
3. acknowledgement of wider programme, software, review and qualification contributors where authorship criteria are not met.

Do not use authorship simply to represent every participating organisation. Conversely, do not omit a contributor whose substantive contribution meets normal authorship criteria merely because the article is an overview.

## Provisional first-author position

Given that the current article is being developed and integrated from the SWAP5 workstream by Ab Veldhuizen, the natural provisional position is Ab Veldhuizen as penvoerder / first author, subject to normal co-author agreement before submission.

## Co-author selection criteria

A prospective co-author should have made a substantive contribution to at least one of:

- scientific continuity and interpretation of SWAP;
- design or qualification of the SWAP5 model basis;
- MODFLOW 6 / iMOD coupling context described in the article;
- MultiSWAP / NHI application context described in the article;
- Ribasim / surface-water context if the article makes non-trivial statements about that component;
- substantial drafting, critical revision or interpretation of the integrated article.

Names should be fixed only after the final technical scope is stable. Do not infer co-authorship from project membership or from the author list of the 2025 SWAP 50-year article.

## Institutional framing

The body text should not present the article as a formal NHI architecture statement unless the relevant programme authority explicitly endorses that status.

Safer framing:

> This article describes the ongoing development from the perspective of the SWAP5 modernization and places it in the broader public Dutch modelling context.

## Author-note action before submission

Before submission:

1. freeze the substantive scope;
2. identify contributors against the criteria above;
3. agree author order explicitly;
4. verify affiliations and names;
5. ask every co-author to approve the publication firewall and the final claims;
6. distinguish author contributions from acknowledgements.
