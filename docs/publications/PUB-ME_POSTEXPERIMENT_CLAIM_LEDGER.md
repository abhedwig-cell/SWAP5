# PUB-ME post-experiment claim ledger

Status: **living manuscript claim boundary after D1-D6**

## Safe empirical claims now

### C1 — structural authority

Within the tested SWAP5 public transaction/state interfaces, selected invalid candidate-to-accepted transitions are structurally blocked before committed scientific state can be changed.

Owned evidence: D1, D3.

### C2 — causal-boundary localization

For the tested accounting, restart, numerical-workspace and external-publication faults, explicit transition-authority checks detect the authority violation before the strong B1 comparator observes its downstream consequence.

Owned evidence: D2, D4, D5, D6.

### C3 — strong conventional qualification still matters

B1 eventually detects every executable D2/D4/D5/D6 seeded fault in the bounded study.

This is a primary result, not an embarrassment to hide.

### C4 — consequence channels differ

The same candidate/accepted distinction matters in multiple non-equivalent scientific-history carriers: accounting, persistence, numerical retry origin and external publication.

This supports transferability of the authority concept across responsibilities inside one model architecture. It does not prove transferability across different scientific models.

## Prohibited wording

Do not write:

- “conventional testing misses these faults”;
- “six independent experiments prove…”;
- “SWAP5 invented transactional simulation/rollback”;
- “transition authority increases correctness by X%”;
- “the method guarantees scientific validity”;
- “SWAP5 is equivalent to SWAP4.3.1” without a scope qualifier;
- “first method” or “unique method” without later systematic literature closure.

## Preferred post-result wording

A bounded formulation consistent with current evidence is:

> The tested transition-authority contracts did not replace conventional scientific-software qualification. Rather, they either made selected invalid transitions unrepresentable or localized authority violations before downstream regression, restart, numerical-continuation or publication checks observed their consequences.

This wording remains subject to the outstanding preservation and replication gates.
