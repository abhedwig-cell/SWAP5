# NH01 eliminated fixed-interface response

Date: 2026-09-21  
Status: RESEARCH DERIVATION, NO PRODUCTION CHANGE

For the NH01 top-system balance,

\[
S_S(z_p-z_{p0}) = W_S - C\Delta t(z_p-H_c),
\]

solve for the internal phreatic state at a trial interface head:

\[
z_p(H_c)=
\frac{S_S z_{p0}+W_S+C\Delta t H_c}
     {S_S+C\Delta t}.
\]

The integrated transfer from the top system to groundwater is

\[
E_c(H_c)=C\Delta t[z_p(H_c)-H_c].
\]

Substitution gives the exact condensed response

\[
E_c(H_c)
=
\beta [W_S+S_S(z_{p0}-H_c)],
\]

with

\[
\beta=\frac{C\Delta t}{S_S+C\Delta t}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\frac{dE_c}{dH_c}=-\beta S_S.
\]

The condensed coupling slope is generated by eliminating an internal top-system state. It is not the interface conductance \(C\), and it is not simply the top-system storage \(S_S\). It contains both through the dimensionless finite-window factor \(\beta\).

The groundwater balance is

\[
S_M(H_c-H_{c0})=W_M+E_c(H_c),
\]

so the exact scalar residual is

\[
F(H_c)=S_M(H_c-H_{c0})-W_M-\beta[W_S+S_S(z_{p0}-H_c)]=0.
\]

Its exact Jacobian is

\[
F'(H_c)=S_M+\beta S_S.
\]

For NH01, \(S_S=S_M=0.10\), \(C=0.20/day\), \(\Delta t=1 day\), hence \(\beta=2/3\),

\[
dE_c/dH_c=-0.0666666666667
\]

and

\[
F'=0.1666666666667.
\]

The root is \(H_c=8.04\) m, \(z_p=8.06\) m, and \(E_c=0.004\) m.

## Limiting interpretation

As \(C\Delta t\to\infty\), \(\beta\to1\), \(z_p-H_c\to0\), and \(dE_c/dH_c\to-S_S\). The eliminated top-system response then contributes its full storage sensitivity to the groundwater residual, whose Jacobian tends to \(S_M+S_S\).

As \(C\Delta t\to0\), \(\beta\to0\) and \(dE_c/dH_c\to0\). The groundwater equation becomes locally insensitive to top-system storage over that finite window because essentially no water crosses the internal top-system resistance during the window.

Thus \(C\), \(S_S\), and the condensed response magnitude \(\beta S_S\) are mathematically related but physically different objects.

## Relation to production u/q_u

No identity with production \(u\) is claimed here.

The derivation instead supplies a prospective test. If production \(u/q_u\) is a valid response condensation of the same fixed-interface physics, its affine term must reproduce the eliminated SWAP response around the same origin and finite window after reconciling sign convention, flux versus integrated-volume units, and the additional internal states of real SWAP.

The production formulation must be mapped onto these equations; these equations must not be reinterpreted to fit production.
