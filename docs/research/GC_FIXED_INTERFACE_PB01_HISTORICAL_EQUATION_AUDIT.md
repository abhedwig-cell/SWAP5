## Exact algebra from the historical predictor definition

Production source states:

    u   = dt / (dH_bot/dq_bot)
    q_u = u*(H_end-H_start)/dt - q_bot

where native q_bot is positive INTO SWAP.

For the NH01 predictor:

    H_end-H_start = W_S/S_S + q_bot*(dt/S_S + 1/C)

and

    u = dt/(dt/S_S + 1/C).

Substitution gives:

    q_u = (u/S_S)*(W_S/dt).

The q_bot term cancels exactly.

This historical q_u is therefore a predictor-derived balance intercept. It is not, by this equation alone, the physical outward bottom flux at H_end.

## Correct affine reconstruction of native q_bot

The inverse predictor relation is:

    q_bot(H)
      = q_bot,* + (u/dt)*(H-H_*).

This has POSITIVE slope because q_bot is positive INTO SWAP.

The corresponding physical groundwater-directed exchange is:

    q_out(H) = -q_bot(H)
             = -q_bot,* - (u/dt)*(H-H_*),

with NEGATIVE slope.

Therefore if q_u is used as the intercept of a groundwater-directed exchange law, the physically consistent affine form must have a negative tangent under the documented q_bot sign.
