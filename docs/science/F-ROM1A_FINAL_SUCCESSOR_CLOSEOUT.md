# ROM-1A final successor closeout

ROM-1A is closed under successor research Reference authority.

The original 12-history design was never changed. Numerical blockers were handled
through explicit diagnostic and qualification workunits, not by retuning histories,
timestep or physical forcing.

The final R2 library contains 768 accepted B01 states: 512 discovery and 256 held-out.
The discovery-qualified Reference policy was frozen before H01-H04 were exposed.
Held-out execution required 20 fallbacks, all already-qualified total-only cases; no
new local-balance class appeared and no policy was changed from held-out outcomes.

B14 remains sealed for the later material-transfer gate.

ROM-1B is now authorized. ROM-1B must preserve the data firewall: discovery histories
may propose reduced-state coordinates; H01-H04 may evaluate predictive sufficiency but
may not be used iteratively to enrich the coordinate. B14 remains unavailable until
the preregistered transfer stage.
