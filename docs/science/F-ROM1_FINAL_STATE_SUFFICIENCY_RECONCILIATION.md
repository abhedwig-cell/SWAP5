## Scope and governing authority

ROM-1 is adjudicated against the governing research proposition in `docs/science/F-ROM_RESEARCH_PROPOSITION.md`.

That proposition permits ROM-2 only after a useful reduced state:
1. materially reduces predictive ambiguity above the Reference floor;
2. survives held-out histories/probes;
3. **transfers across the preregistered contrasting-material challenge**; and
4. is not merely equivalent to an equally effective simple coarse-Richards representation.

The proposition explicitly lists `MATERIAL_SPECIFIC_ONLY` as a valid negative outcome.
