# F-ROM-LARE BC2-C4J closeout

C4J closes with a split result. The formal aggregate decision remains C4J_DIAGNOSTIC_BLOCKED because the preregistered root-multiplicity cross-quadrature gate fails, but the independent 5 cm direction-geometry diagnostic is supported.

The apparent second 2.5 cm root at 64-point quadrature is not reproduced at 128 points. At 64 points the two clusters differ by only about 4.10e-7 cm/d in qi and 6.90e-7 cm/d in qH. At 128 points all four frozen starts collapse to one cluster. This is treated as numerical root-resolution sensitivity, not evidence that Wb/Wt/H physically admit two distinct quadratic profiles.

The 5 cm direction result is robust. Under WT_FALL the quadratic reconstruction reduces interface-slope RMS from about 0.0221 to 0.0138 and water-table-slope RMS from about 0.0329 to 0.0256. Under WT_RISE it increases those RMS errors from about 0.0342 to 0.0631 and from about 0.0472 to 0.0642, respectively. Under WT_HOLD the baseline slope error is essentially numerical zero while the quadratic profile introduces finite slope error.

Thus the transferable finding is direction-dependent gradient geometry, not quadratic branch multiplicity. No branch selector, direction switch or replacement closure is authorized.
