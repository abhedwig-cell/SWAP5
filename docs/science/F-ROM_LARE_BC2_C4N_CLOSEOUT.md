# F-ROM-LARE BC2-C4N closeout

Formal decision: FACE_INTERPOLATED_GRADIENT_MIXED.

C4N tested a parameter-free face interpolation of the B9 terminal and bulk slope proxies, with frozen correction delta_s = d/(B+d)*(b_bulk-a_t). Interface K and qH remained B9 and all hard gates passed.

The candidate helps both FALL cases. At 2.5 cm, qi RMS decreases from about 0.294 to 0.232 cm/d. At 5 cm, it decreases from about 0.492 to 0.331 cm/d. The correction has the same sign as the Reference-required correction for about 84.4% and 96.5% of intervals respectively.

It is not transferable to RISE. At 2.5 cm RISE RMS increases from about 0.361 to 0.373 cm/d. At 5 cm RISE it increases from about 0.630 to 1.035 cm/d, and the correction sign matches Reference on only 18.8% of intervals. HOLD is also damaged, producing artificial qi of about 0.0223 and 0.0439 cm/d at 2.5 and 5 cm.

Thus neither direct central weighting nor opposite-distance face weighting provides a universal memoryless gradient correction. No direction switch or propagation is authorized. C4K already showed that RISE and FALL occupy different regions of the existing H/Wb/Wt state space, so the next bounded diagnostic maps the Reference-required correction against those existing state coordinates before proposing another closure.
