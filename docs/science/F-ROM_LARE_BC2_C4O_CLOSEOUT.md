# F-ROM-LARE BC2-C4O closeout

Formal decision: NO_SINGLE_EXISTING_STATE_CONDITIONER_DOMINATES.

C4O tested three zero-fit signals derived only from the existing B9 state: the C4N face geometry signal, lower-boundary disequilibrium, and their interaction. No regression, fitted coefficient, direction label, Hdot or extra state was used.

The interaction fixes one important defect: under HOLD it collapses essentially to zero at both widths. But it is not transferable across representation width. At 2.5 cm, P_FACE remains clearly stronger in pooled moving data, with signed cosine about 0.402 and sign agreement 83.2%; P_INTERACTION gives about -0.073 and 51.2%. At 5 cm, the ranking reverses: P_INTERACTION gives signed cosine about 0.306 and sign agreement 88.9%, while P_FACE gives about -0.082 and 57.6%.

That width reversal is the decisive result. A new scalar blend or switch would be post-hoc tuning rather than an established physical closure. The LARE branch therefore returns to representation selection.

The next bounded task is to reconcile the already existing LARE and CoRichards representation options against the accumulated state-sufficiency and closure evidence before proposing any further model family.
