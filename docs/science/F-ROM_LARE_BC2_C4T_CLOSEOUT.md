# F-ROM-LARE BC2-C4T closeout

Formal decision: **C4T_LARE_PURPOSE_VALUE_SIGNAL_RESOLVED**.

C4T combines the blind C4R fidelity evidence with the full C4S same-workload comparator cohort and a preregistered position-balanced shared-host timing screen. All compiled-equivalence and checksum gates pass. The measurement remains a screening experiment: the host is not admitted for a formal portable CPU baseline.

The R16 calibration selected 64 complete four-history workload repetitions. Each of the 15 routes then occupied every timing position exactly twice across 30 measured rounds.

Mean CPU per complete four-history workload was approximately 0.01104 s for R16. LARE R3, R4, R5, R6 and R8 were respectively about 0.415x, 0.517x, 0.634x, 0.749x and 0.984x the R16 mean and each had a resolved paired two-SE cost advantage. LARE R12 was about 1.443x R16 and resolved more expensive.

This must be read together with fidelity. C4R showed R4 as the blind minimum LARE dimension crossing both R2 and FMC on the groundwater vector. R12, not R4-R8, was required to cross FMC when mapped 10-cm profile fidelity was included. Therefore state reduction is strongly purpose dependent.

CoRichards R3-R12 is numerically available on this fixed-water-table workload, but its complete admitted route including fallback is resolved more expensive than R16 in C4T. Higher-dimensional CoRichards is nevertheless hydrologically much closer to R16 than corresponding LARE members. FMC is also more expensive than R16 in this C4T screen. These are workload-specific results and do not supersede separately governed performance experiments with different workloads or implementations.

The C4T result establishes a **relative cost-fidelity value signal**, not application acceptance. There is still no independently sourced numerical envelope saying which storage, recharge, bottom-flux, sign/reversal or profile errors are acceptable for an actual groundwater application.

The next authority is therefore purpose-dependent groundwater acceptance-envelope binding. No new LARE closure, production ROM, portable speedup claim or application admission is authorized here.
