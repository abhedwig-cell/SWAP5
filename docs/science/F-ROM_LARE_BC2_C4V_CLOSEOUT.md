Formal decision: **C4V_HIGHER_DIMENSION_GW_FRONTIER_PERSISTS**.

C4V extends the already blind C4R fixed-water-table histories prospectively from 0.064 day to 1.024 day. All prefix, provenance, D13-FMC reproduction, pre-Reference FMC-domain, transaction-mass and fail-closed gates pass.

The short-horizon R4 result does not persist. Using the preregistered six-component groundwater vector, R8 and R12 are the only reduced LARE members that remain no worse than both R2 and FMC at every prospective checkpoint 0.128, 0.256, 0.512 and 1.024 day. R5 loses the frontier after 0.256 day; R6 loses it at the 1.024-day checkpoint.

At 1.024 day, pooled LARE R8 errors are about 0.00921 cm for storage and cumulative bottom exchange, 0.0375 cm/d for terminal bottom flux, one sign error, 0.0205 cm/d absolute mean signed bottom-flux bias, and 0.0327 cm maximum absolute final cumulative-exchange error. R12 is closer again, with about 0.00237 cm storage/cumulative RMSE and 0.0238 cm/d bottom-flux RMSE.

The state requirement is therefore horizon dependent. R4 is sufficient only for the demonstrated short C4R comparator-relative GW frontier; R8 is the minimum demonstrated member for the one-day C4V frontier. This shift is caused by accumulated direction/bias behavior, not by mass-balance failure or numerical invalidity.

Profile/process fidelity remains a different purpose. R8 mapped-theta RMSE at 1.024 day is about 0.00810, while R12 is about 0.00137 and the R16 LARE operator control about 3.44e-5. More groundwater fidelity therefore does not erase the purpose-dependent profile-information requirement.
