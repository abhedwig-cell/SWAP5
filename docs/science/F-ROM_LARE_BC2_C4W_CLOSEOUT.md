# F-ROM-LARE BC2-C4W closeout

Formal decision: **C4W_SPURIOUS_REVERSAL_MAGNITUDE_CHARACTERIZED**.

C4W reexecutes the frozen C4V LARE members against the immutable C4V Reference trajectory only to characterize the magnitude of the long-horizon sign mismatches. It defines no near-zero deadband and cannot change the C4V decision.

The V03 failures of R4, R5 and R6 are not near-zero artifacts. Their first wrong-sign steps occur at 0.112, 0.295 and 0.629 day while the Reference bottom flux is about -0.992, -0.596 and -0.373 cm/d, respectively. The mismatch then persists through the 1.024-day endpoint. R8, R12 and R16 have no V03 sign mismatch.

V04 is different: the one-step early reversal occurs where the Reference flux magnitude is only about 0.0029 cm/d. C4W reports that contrast but does not convert it into an acceptance threshold.

Therefore the C4V one-day minimum R8 remains robust to the hypothesis that the lower-dimensional sign failures were merely an arbitrary zero-crossing classification artifact. The next open scientific axis is independently preregistered material/constitutive transfer or lower-boundary transfer.
