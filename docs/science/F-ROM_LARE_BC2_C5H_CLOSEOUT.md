# F-ROM-LARE BC2-C5H closeout

C5H extends both R512 and R1024 to T8 under the unchanged B14 dynamic prescribed-head history and numerical policy.

Temporal convergence strengthens: T4-T8 bottom-flux RMSE is about 0.00271 cm/d at R512 and 0.00340 at R1024. The descriptive temporal order rises to about 0.79 and 0.84 respectively.

Temporal error is now strongly subdominant to the matched spatial gap, at about 12% for R512 and 15% for R1024.

Yet the R512-R1024 spatial gap still increases as time is refined: 0.01928 cm/d at T1, 0.02107 at T2, 0.02233 at T4 and 0.02303 at T8. The T4-T8 increase is smaller than before, about 3.1%, but it is still systematic.

C5I therefore performs one final matched temporal refinement to T16 at both spatial resolutions before any R2048 extension. No LARE or closure change is authorized.
