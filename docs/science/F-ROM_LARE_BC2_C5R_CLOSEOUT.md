# F-ROM-LARE BC2-C5R closeout

C5R is the final vertical-memory-breadth discriminator before reopening propagation closure.

On fresh R01-R04 B14 dynamic prescribed-head histories, Reference temporal quality passes. D12 itself preserves the B10 -> B5 -> B2P5 bottom-focus progression, but D12_B2P5 does not cross the independent R1024 comparator.

The decisive comparison is D12_B2P5 versus D8_B2P5. Both have identical 5/2.5/2.5-cm lower-boundary support and unchanged CURRENT_LAYER_FACE closure; D12 adds four vertically distributed states above 110 cm. Their groundwater responses are numerically indistinguishable: bottom-flux, storage, cumulative exchange, signed-bias, sign and reversal metrics are effectively identical. The mapped-profile difference is negligible.

Thus the C5P residual is no longer plausibly explained by missing broad vertical state information. Bottom placement remains causally important, but once the tested B2P5 support is present, adding D8 -> D12 state breadth does not rescue high-resolution fidelity.

The remaining error is therefore much more strongly identified as a propagation/inter-layer closure limitation. This does not reopen scalar tuning: C4H-C4O remain authoritative against conductivity-only and scalar/current-state gradient fixes. C5S is read-only derivation of genuinely distinct conservative closure families before any implementation.
