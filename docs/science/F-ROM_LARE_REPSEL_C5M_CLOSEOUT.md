# F-ROM-LARE C5M representation evidence reconciliation

C5M is a read-only synthesis after the C5B-C5L Reference audit.

The robust conclusion is that vertical placement is an independent representation axis. Lower-zone-focused representations outperform equal-dimension uniform controls across B14 fixed-head, B01 dynamic prescribed-head and B14 dynamic prescribed-head evidence. C5C additionally shows strong sensitivity to bottom-adjacent layer thickness.

The numerical minimum dimensions reported earlier are not portable. C4R, C4X, C4Z and C5A place their formal frontiers at different dimensions because material, lower-boundary semantics, purpose and comparator strength differ. C5C-C5L also show that the historical R16 dynamic-head comparator was not spatially stable enough to support an absolute sufficiency claim.

Therefore C5M retains the placement hypothesis but retires any claim that 3, 4, 8 or 12 layers is a universal minimum.

The next necessary experiment is C5N: fresh B14 dynamic prescribed-head histories, the already-frozen R3/R4/R5/R6/R8/R12 layer ladder and P4/U4/U8 placement controls, and paired R1024/R2048 T16 Reference trajectories. Candidate error must be reported against both Reference levels, with R1024-R2048 disagreement carried separately as Reference numerical uncertainty rather than used as a pass/fail tolerance.

No new layer geometry is designed in C5M. Application acceptance remains blocked by C4U, and no performance, speed or production-ROM claim is authorized.
