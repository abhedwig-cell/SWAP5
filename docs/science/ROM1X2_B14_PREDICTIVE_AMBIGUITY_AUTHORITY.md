# ROM-1X2 B14 predictive ambiguity authority

X1 found 351 B14 hidden-state collisions for the frozen Z8+G8 coordinate. X2 does
not use those outcomes to redesign the coordinate. It asks only whether the first
eight collision pairs, frozen before any B14 future probe, are predictively
distinguishable under identical forcing.

The probe set is not new: HOLD, TOP_PLUS, BOTTOM_HEAD_RISE and BOTTOM_HEAD_FALL at
4, 16 and 64 steps were frozen in F-ROM1A before material-transfer outcomes and were
already used for the B01 discovery ambiguity experiment.

For each trajectory, future response is Y(t)-Y(0). Pair ambiguity is the absolute
difference of those responses. Formal outputs are total storage, 0-40 cm storage and
40-160 cm storage.

B14 pairwise response scales are twice the pre-ROM1 single-trajectory Reference
floors. No B14 probe outcome contributes to them:
- total storage: 7.105427357601002e-14 cm;
- 0-40 cm storage: 4.8537174279772444e-11 cm;
- 40-160 cm storage: 4.850164714298444e-11 cm.

Any exceedance establishes predictive ambiguity for material transfer. The result may
reject transfer but may not be used to enrich Z8+G8 on B14.
