Independent review note:
The +1.d-10 term in Lev2Comp is equation-level legacy semantics. It means that at an internal compartment bottom and for levels up to 1e-10 cm below that bottom, legacy still identifies the upper compartment. This can yield dzcpwlevsat equal to zero or slightly negative in that seam. F-VQ45 treats this as a separately tested scientific/numerical boundary seam rather than silently removing it.
