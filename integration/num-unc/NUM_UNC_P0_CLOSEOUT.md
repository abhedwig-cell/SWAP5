# NUM-UNC P0 closeout\n\nDate: 2026-09-19\nDecision: CLOSED_NEGATIVE_PRIMARY_MECHANISM\n\n## What P0 tested\n\nP0 was deliberately designed as a kill-pilot, not as a demonstration. The target claim required more than timestep sensitivity or a threshold flip. A hydrological transition had to amplify differences between independently admissible temporal realizations enough to change a preregistered scientific inference.\n\nThree distinct transition families were screened with the same Reference Richards route and the independently inherited N0/N1 timestep pair.\n\n## Evidence\n\n### C: surface flux to ponding\n\nThe corrected N0 locator and independent spatial-control gate produced a stable transition neighbourhood. At the frozen centre, N0 produced a ponding event and N1 did not. The inference therefore changed. However the preregistered water-content amplification statistic was only A=1.144257873, and post-transition divergence did not exceed twice the no-switch floor. C is therefore THRESHOLD_PROXIMITY_ONLY.\n\nThat distinction matters: C shows that a binary event label can depend on an admissible temporal realization when the physical case is deliberately placed next to the event threshold. It does not show dynamic hydrological amplification of numerical uncertainty.\n\n### B: lower-boundary exchange reversal\n\nAll five preregistered prescribed-head offsets remained NET_DRAINAGE. The declared envelope was not widened after inspection. B therefore closed as NO_TRANSITION_IN_DECLARED_ENVELOPE and never progressed to N1.\n\n### A: drought-stress timing\n\nHistorical A14/A22 evidence supplied provenance for the five Hupsel simple-maize Feddes parameters and the potential-transpiration anchor. A controlled B01 stress transition was located with N0 and the frozen outer bracket survived a twofold vertical-grid refinement.\n\nIn A1, all six N0/N1 routes were admissible. A- remained STRESS_BEFORE_RESCUE, A+ remained NOT_BEFORE_RESCUE, and the transition-centre A0 remained STRESS_BEFORE_RESCUE in both temporal realizations. Its first stress time was 0.4032 day in both N0 and N1.\n\nContinuous trajectory divergence did grow around onset and rescue, but only to A=1.661471061. The post-transition divergence also remained below twice the preregistered A+ numerical floor. A is therefore INVARIANT for the primary inference.\n\n## Decision against salvage\n\nThe P0 go rule is not met. The only inference flip in the pilot is the C surface-event flip, and that flip was preregistered as threshold-proximity-only rather than amplification. This activates the spirit and wording of kill criterion K3: observed inference changes do not establish the proposed amplification mechanism.\n\nConsequently:\n\n- no O14 replication is run, because there is no qualifying B01 mechanism to replicate;\n- no P0b convergence-tolerance factor is added;\n- the B search envelope is not expanded;\n- transition cases are not relocated;\n- the amplification threshold or numerical floor is not weakened.\n\nThese are scientific closure rules, not resource constraints.\n\n## What can still be claimed\n\nThe pilot supports a narrow methodological observation: an admissible timestep change can alter a deliberately transition-near ponding-event classification without producing the stronger dynamical amplification that motivated NUM-UNC. In the separate controlled drought-stress experiment, the same temporal pair changed continuous trajectories and some event timing at the outer cases by one half-step, but did not change the preregistered before/after-rescue inference.\n\nThe evidence does not support the converse universal claim that numerical amplification cannot occur in vadose-zone modelling. The tested mechanisms, soil, forcing designs and temporal pair are limited.\n\n## Publication consequence\n\nThe standalone NUM-UNC paper should not be advanced under its current amplification-based novelty claim. Doing so would require turning a negative pilot into a positive story by post-hoc search, which the protocol was designed to prevent.\n\nThe results can remain useful as negative research evidence, as a robustness appendix or methodological example in another paper, or as motivation for a genuinely new hypothesis that is justified independently before new simulations are inspected.\n
