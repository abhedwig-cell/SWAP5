# TRACE Exposure Batch 01 closeout

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: `CLOSED`

## Design

Exposure Batch 01 was selected and persisted before detailed inspection. It contained six scientific elements, three in SWAP and three in ANIMO, stratified across process/constitutive relations, boundary or transfer relations, and numerical-scientific conventions.

Historical/pilot discrepancies were excluded from confirmatory counting by protocol.

## Prospective results

| Model | Elements exposed | Candidate triggers | Confirmed discrepancies | Excluded candidates | Closed null elements |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SWAP | 3 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 3 |
| ANIMO | 3 | 2 | 0 | 2 | 3 |
| **Total** | **6** | **2** | **0** | **2** | **6** |

The two prospectively registered candidates were:

- `TRACE-ANIMO-0001`: apparent conflict between a retained pre-review TCD-015 disposition and later admission state. Excluded after reconstruction showed an explicit predecessor/review/successor authority sequence.
- `TRACE-ANIMO-0002`: apparent conflict between a GHG03 qualification document labelled review-pending and later qualified/admitted status. Excluded after reconstruction showed that the document is deliberately immutable pre-review authoring evidence under an explicit freeze contract.

Both candidates were registered and their pre-resolution evidence frozen before the exclusion mechanism was investigated.

## What Batch 01 establishes

Batch 01 demonstrates that the prospective machinery is operational:

1. denominator elements can be locked before inspection;
2. candidate registration can occur before resolution;
3. pre-resolution evidence can be preserved;
4. apparent inconsistencies can be excluded rather than promoted when authority/provenance evidence resolves them;
5. null observations remain visible in the denominator.

The dominant lesson from the two candidate triggers is methodological: repository representations with different status values cannot be treated as a discrepancy until their temporal role, freeze semantics and successor authority are reconstructed.

## What Batch 01 does not establish

The batch does **not** estimate a population prevalence of theory-documentation-code discrepancies.

Reasons:

- only six elements were exposed;
- selection was planned and stratified, not random;
- the two repositories have different documentation/governance structures;
- no confirmed prospective discrepancy occurred;
- candidate-trigger frequency is not discrepancy frequency.

The observed `2/6` candidate-trigger rate is therefore descriptive workflow evidence only. The confirmed-discrepancy count is `0/6`, which is likewise too small and too design-specific for a general prevalence conclusion.

## Protocol consequence

No protocol or codebook amendment is required after Batch 01.

The existing protocol already requires authority reconstruction and allows exclusion. Temporal provenance remains open-coded as an exclusion mechanism rather than being promoted into a new discrepancy relation class from confirmatory data.

The v0.2 codebook remains frozen for the next exposure batch.

## Next exposure

Batch 02 must again be selected and persisted before detailed inspection. To increase scientific coverage without result-driven selection, it should use the same three broad strata but select different named surfaces than Batch 01.

No Batch 02 element may be selected because it is suspected to contain a discrepancy.
